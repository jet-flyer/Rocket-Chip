## 1. Header note: PICS rule used in this record

- CCSDS records partial conformance in a PICS proforma in Annex A. In section 2, these Blue Books have one: 211.0-B-6, 211.1-B-4, 211.2-B-3, 133.0-B-2 and 732.1-B-3 (pages in section 3). 232.1-B-2 has no PICS annex (COV line 174).
- 211.1-B-4 A2.1.4 NOTE (p.A-3): "A YES answer means that the implementation does not conform to the Recommended Standard. Non-supported mandatory capabilities are to be identified in the PICS, with an explanation of why the implementation is non-conforming."
- 133.0-B-2 A1.3 (quoted in CONF line 59): "If a mandatory requirement is not satisfied, exception information must be supplied by entering a reference Xi, where i is a unique identifier, to an accompanying rationale for the noncompliance."
- A20.1-Y-1 (Yellow, a procedure for CCSDS writers of PICS proformas; it does not bind an implementer) gives the same shape. §3.2.4.2 (p.3-2): "Non-supported mandatory capabilities are to be identified in the ICS with an explanation of why the implementation is non-conforming." §3.2.3 (p.3-1): "The issue(s) of the specification(s) to be supported shall be stated in the proforma." Section 2 states the issues for this reason. (Duke GOY 2.19.)
- 211.1-B-4 A1.2 (p.A-2) status key: "O optional." and "O.<n> optional, but support of at least one of the group of options labeled by the same numeral <n> is required." So an O item can be skipped, and an O.<n> group needs one item. A skipped mandatory (M) item means "does not conform". It needs a row with a reason. Section 3 gives one row for each M item that Starcom skips or covers in part, plus notable O items.

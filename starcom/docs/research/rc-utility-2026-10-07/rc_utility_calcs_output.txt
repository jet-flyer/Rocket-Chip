  coh BPSK: uncoded Eb/N0 shift from BER 1e-3 to 1% PER(69 B) = 2.52 dB (model)
(Monte Carlo, 1500 frames per point, bisection; treat as +/-0.3 dB.)

             130.1-G-3 4.5 p.4-7: hard decision loses > 2 dB vs soft.

S21 GPS L1 Doppler, Doppler rate, PLL jerk limit, oscillator g-sensitivity (v2c)
==============================================================================
  L1 wavelength 0.1903 m ; Doppler per m/s of line-of-sight speed = f/c = 5.2550 Hz per m/s
    300 m/s line of sight ->  1576.5 Hz
    500 m/s line of sight ->  2627.5 Hz
    515 m/s line of sight ->  2706.3 Hz
    600 m/s line of sight ->  3153.0 Hz
  Doppler rate per 1 g of line-of-sight acceleration: 51.53 Hz/s ; 4 g -> 206.1 Hz/s ; 40 m/s^2 -> 210.2 Hz/s ; 20 m/s^3 jerk -> 105.1 Hz/s^2
  Third-order PLL steady-state jerk error: theta_e = (d3R/dt3) / w0^3, with w0 = Bn / 0.7845 (Kaplan form;
  reproduces Ebinuma & Kato 2012: 25 Hz -> about 78 g/s). Threshold: theta_e <= 45 deg = lambda/8.
   Bn 10 Hz: w0 12.75 rad/s -> max LOS jerk    49.3 m/s^3 =   5.0 g/s (jerk term alone, no noise)
   Bn 15 Hz: w0 19.12 rad/s -> max LOS jerk   166.3 m/s^3 =  17.0 g/s (jerk term alone, no noise)
   Bn 18 Hz: w0 22.94 rad/s -> max LOS jerk   287.3 m/s^3 =  29.3 g/s (jerk term alone, no noise)
   Bn 25 Hz: w0 31.87 rad/s -> max LOS jerk   769.8 m/s^3 =  78.5 g/s (jerk term alone, no noise)
  Example jerk (NOT customer data): 0 -> 10 g in 0.1 s = 981 m/s^3 = 100 g/s along the line of sight
  Oscillator g-sensitivity: df = Gamma * a * f_L1. Gamma = 1e-9 /g -> 1.575 Hz per g (= 0.300 m/s apparent LOS speed per g)
